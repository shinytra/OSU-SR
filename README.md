# OSU SR

A fork of [osu!lazer](https://github.com/ppy/osu) focused on continuous play and skillset training:
play a chain of the hardest segments of your local maps, picked for a chosen skillset, with smooth transitions.

Status: early prototype (osu!standard only). See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the plan and conventions,
and [README.lazer.md](README.lazer.md) for the original osu!lazer README.
